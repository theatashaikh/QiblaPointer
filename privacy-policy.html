Qibla Pointer Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

What the app does

Qibla Pointer is a Qibla direction compass. It uses your current GPS location or a city you select from an offline list to calculate the direction and distance to the Kaaba in Mecca.

Location data

The app requests foreground-only device location permission. Your location is used on the device to calculate the Qibla bearing and distance. The app stores the last valid location or the city you selected locally on your device so it can restore your position when you reopen the app offline.

The app does not perform background location tracking.

Compass and sensor data

Your device heading (compass sensor) is read locally to point the compass needle. Heading data is not sent to any server.

City search

City search uses a bundled offline city list that ships with the app. It does not use an online geocoder or any network service.

Data sharing

  • Your location data is not sold or shared with third parties.
  • The app does not require account creation.
  • The app does not display advertisements.
  • The app does not process payments.
  • The app does not include third-party advertising SDKs.

Error monitoring

The app may optionally use CatDoes Watch for error monitoring. When enabled, CatDoes Watch may receive crash reports, error reports, and limited device and app context to help diagnose issues.

If CatDoes Watch is not enabled in a given build, no monitoring data is collected or sent.

Retention and deletion

Location data stored by the app (your last GPS position or selected city) resides locally on your device. You can remove it by:

  • Uninstalling the app, or
  • Clearing the app's data in your device settings.

To reset the selected city and return to your current GPS location, use the current GPS location action within the app.

If error monitoring is enabled, any monitoring data is retained according to the monitoring service's policies.

Children's privacy

Qibla Pointer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data to the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Security

The app stores location data locally on your device and does not transmit it over the network. Standard device-level protections (screen lock, encryption) apply to data at rest on your device.
